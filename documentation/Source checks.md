# Source checks

Checked on 6 November 2026, with a second pass the same day for revision 3 · companion to [Stellar-Forge Build Plan](Stellar-Forge%20Build%20Plan.md)

All 56 references in the plan match their published records, and 57 claims from the plan and the outside review were checked against the papers, the MIST website, Aion Forge's code or a recomputation. 36 hold as written, 13 needed tightening, 6 were wrong, and 2 wait for later data. The "Plan now says" column gives the plan's current wording for every claim that changed.

These checks confirm what each source says. They do not show that a model suits Stellar-Forge; numerical tests and scientific validation still happen in each phase.

## How the checks were done

- **References:** each was matched by journal, volume, page and year through Crossref; the three without DOIs (Chandrasekhar 1939, Zahn 1977, Verbunt & Phinney 1995) matched in OpenAlex. Metadata and retrieved abstracts are in `references/metadata/crossref_and_abstracts.json`.
- **Claims:** abstracts came from Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex; full texts of 22 papers came from arXiv (`references/papers/`, with plain-text extracts in `references/papers/text/`); MIST facts came from its website (`references/web/`); Aion Forge's escape model was read in place, without changing anything.
- **Numbers:** Lane–Emden constants, the Eddington temperature, the IMF share and the habitable-zone timing were recomputed with SciPy (`references/scripts/numeric_checks.py`).
- **Reproducing:** `references/scripts/` holds the lookup and search scripts used; run them with `python3 -I` and an output folder outside `references/`.
